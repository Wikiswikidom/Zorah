# Content Guidelines

Voice: refined, confident, warm and craft-led. Avoid exaggerated luxury clichés and unsupported claims.

Product copy should answer what it is, why it matters, materials, dimensions, capacity/what fits, care, delivery and returns.

Photography should prioritize clean product shots plus authentic craftsmanship imagery. Alt text should describe the useful visual content, not stuff keywords.

CTAs should be direct: Shop the Collection, Discover the Craft, Create Yours, Add to Bag, Continue to Checkout.

Content editors must preserve brand terminology and approved product facts.
