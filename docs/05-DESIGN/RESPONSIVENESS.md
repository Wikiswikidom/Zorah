# Responsiveness

Design mobile-first, then expand for tablet and desktop. Primary breakpoints should be driven by layout needs rather than device names.

Mobile requirements: sticky or easily reachable navigation, large tap targets, readable product information, simple filter controls, optimized galleries and minimal blocking content.

Desktop may introduce editorial split layouts, larger imagery and richer hover states, but must not create a different information architecture.

Test at narrow Android widths, common iOS widths, tablet and large desktop. Avoid horizontal overflow and layout shifts caused by media loading.
