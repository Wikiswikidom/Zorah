# UI/UX Requirements

Zorah should feel premium, editorial and confident without becoming visually noisy. The landing page and ecommerce experience must feel like one product, not two disconnected sites.

Priorities: product photography, whitespace, typography, clear hierarchy, intuitive navigation, fast discovery and strong mobile usability.

Primary navigation should expose Shop and Collections clearly, with search, wishlist and cart accessible. The landing page must link naturally into commerce through collection/product CTAs.

Avoid excessive gradients, floating cards, particles, oversized animations and generic AI-template patterns. Motion should support hierarchy and storytelling, not compete with products.

Every critical flow must be usable by touch on small screens.
