# Tables

Planned tables and responsibilities:

- `profiles` — customer/admin profile data.
- `roles`, `permissions`, `role_permissions`, `user_roles` — RBAC.
- `addresses` — saved customer delivery addresses.
- `products`, `product_variants`, `product_media` — catalog.
- `categories`, `collections`, `product_categories` — discovery taxonomy.
- `inventory` — stock state and adjustments.
- `carts`, `cart_items`, `wishlists`, `wishlist_items` — customer shopping state.
- `orders`, `order_items`, `payments` — commerce transactions.
- `delivery_rules` — configurable delivery logic.
- `custom_orders`, `custom_order_events` — bespoke workflow.
- `reviews` — customer reviews and moderation state.
- `content_entries`, `promotions` — CMS/marketing.
- `notifications` — user/admin messages.
- `audit_logs` — security/operational history.

Final columns and constraints are defined during Phase 4 schema implementation.
