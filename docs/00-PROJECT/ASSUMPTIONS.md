# Assumptions

- Zorah owns the brand, product photography and product copy used on the site.
- Initial operational market is Nigeria, with Lagos as an important local SEO and delivery market.
- Paystack is the initial payment gateway.
- Delivery is the only fulfilment method at launch.
- Supabase is the system of record for relational application data.
- Product images are stored in managed object storage with validation and size limits.
- Admin staff require different permissions rather than a shared super-admin login.
- Product variants may have different colors, inventory and media.
- Exact delivery zones, prices, return windows and business contact details remain configurable decisions.

Any assumption that becomes false must be recorded in the decision log and reflected in affected architecture documents.
