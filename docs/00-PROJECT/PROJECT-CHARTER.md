# Project Charter

## Vision
Create a premium digital home for Zorah that turns craftsmanship into brand value and makes buying or commissioning a leather handbag simple.

## Objectives
- Establish a strong editorial landing page.
- Convert discovery into an excellent ecommerce journey.
- Support standard and custom handbags.
- Provide secure customer and admin experiences.
- Give Zorah staff control over products, content, orders and operations.

## Primary users
Customers in Nigeria and international visitors; Zorah administrators; catalog/order/content/support staff.

## Success criteria
Fast mobile experience, clear navigation, trustworthy checkout, accurate inventory/order state, manageable CMS, strong SEO and maintainable architecture.

## Constraints
Delivery-only model; prices and delivery rules must be configurable; no secrets in client code; no Firebase/Supabase duplication; mobile-first implementation.
