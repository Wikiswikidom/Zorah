# Requirements

## Functional
Users must browse, search, filter, view, wishlist, cart and purchase products; manage accounts and addresses; track orders; submit custom handbag requests; receive status notifications. Admins must manage catalog, inventory, orders, customers, content, promotions and audit records according to role.

## Non-functional
Mobile-first, accessible, responsive, performant, secure, SEO-friendly, observable and maintainable. Public pages should remain usable on constrained Nigerian mobile connections.

## Business
Delivery only. Delivery prices, availability, promotional rules and product stock must be configurable rather than hard-coded. Custom orders must support review, quotation and conversion into an order.

## Acceptance rule
Every requirement must have a testable acceptance condition before its implementation issue is marked DONE.
