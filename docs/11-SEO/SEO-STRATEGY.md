# SEO Strategy

SEO should attract Nigerian and broader fashion/leather shoppers while building Zorah brand authority.

Priorities: crawlable product/category pages, unique metadata, strong internal linking, useful product copy, high-quality imagery, structured data, clean URLs, sitemap, canonical URLs and fast mobile performance.

Content should naturally target terms around leather handbags, handcrafted bags, Nigerian leather craftsmanship, premium handbags and relevant product/category searches without keyword stuffing.

Brand storytelling and craftsmanship content support long-tail discovery and trust.
