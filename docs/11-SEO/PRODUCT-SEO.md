# Product SEO

Every published product needs a unique title, concise description, canonical URL, appropriate image alt text and structured product data where applicable.

Product copy should include material, design, dimensions, use/capacity, care and availability facts. Variants should have a consistent URL strategy.

Avoid automatically generating thin pages for every filter combination. Related products and collections should provide strong internal links.

When products are discontinued, preserve useful URLs where possible and redirect/archive intelligently.
