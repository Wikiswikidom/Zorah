# Content Strategy

Content pillars:
1. Products and collections.
2. Craftsmanship and leather education.
3. Brand story and design philosophy.
4. Styling/use cases and what-fits-inside guidance.
5. Custom-order stories and behind-the-scenes craft.

Content should support both discovery and conversion. Every editorial page should have a clear next step into a relevant collection/product or inquiry.

Publishing cadence should be determined by business capacity; quality and original imagery matter more than volume.
